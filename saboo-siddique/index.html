UberCare Services

Comprehensive radiology, cardiac and pathology diagnostics for patients, families and referring doctors at Saboo Siddique Hospital, Mumbai.

MRI Scan

High-resolution Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) for detailed evaluation of the brain, spine, joints, soft tissues, and internal organs throughout the body.

CT Scan

Advanced cross-sectional imaging support for trauma, chest, abdomen, head, and other emergency diagnostic requirements.

Full-Body Health Checkups

Comprehensive preventive health screening packages designed to assess and monitor key health parameters in a single visit.

Pathology & Blood Tests

Comprehensive routine and advanced laboratory investigations with seamless digital report management and coordination.

ECG

Quick electrocardiogram (ECG) testing for accurate rhythm assessment and expert doctor-led cardiac evaluation.

2D Echo

Cardiac ultrasound (2D Echo) for detailed assessment of heart structure, valve function, and cardiac pumping efficiency.

TMT/ Stress Test

Exercise-based cardiac stress testing for evaluation of heart function under monitored physical exertion.

Ultrasound/ Sonography

Focused ultrasound evaluations conducted by our experienced Lady Sonologist ensuring comfort, privacy, and precision for every female patient.

Digital X-Ray

Digital X-Ray services for the chest, bones, joints, and other physician/ Gynaecologist-prescribed X-ray examinations.

HSG

Specialized imaging support for gynecological assessment and fertility-related evaluations.

EEG

Electroencephalography (EEG) services for recording brain electrical activity as part of neurologist-recommended evaluations.

EMG

Electrophysiological testing services for assessment of muscle and nerve function in selected neurological conditions.

NCV

Nerve conduction velocity (NCV) testing for evaluation of peripheral nerve disorders and neuromuscular conditions.

Mammography

Breast imaging services for screening and physician-prescribed diagnostic evaluation.
